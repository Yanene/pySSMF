# Slave-spin mean-field theory

This page derives the equations solved by `pySSMF`. It follows the $Z_2$ slave-spin formulation of de' Medici and collaborators [@de2005orbital;@de2017modeling] and its variational extension to symmetry-broken phases [@crispino2023slave]. Throughout, we work at zero temperature. The last section maps each step onto the code.

## Notation

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $i, j$ | lattice sites |
| $m, m'$ | orbital indices |
| $\sigma$ | spin ($\uparrow, \downarrow$) |
| $\nu$ | sublattice index within a supercell ($\nu=A, B$)|
| $d^{\dagger}_{im\sigma}$, $d_{im\sigma}$ | physical electron operators |
| $f^{\dagger}_{im\sigma}$, $f_{im\sigma}$ | auxiliary fermion operators |
| $S^{\alpha}_{im\sigma}$ | slave-spin operators ($\alpha = x, y, z, \pm$) |
| $O_{im\sigma}$ | operator carrying the electron's charge in the slave-spin sector |
| $c_{im\sigma}$ | gauge parameter in the definition of $O_{im\sigma}$ |
| $t^{mm'}_{ij}$ | hopping amplitudes, real and $t_{ii}^{mm'}=0$ |
| $\mu$ | chemical potential |
| $U$, $U'$, $J$ | intra-orbital repulsion, inter-orbital repulsion, Hund's coupling |
| $\lambda_{im\sigma}$ | Lagrange multiplier enforcing the constraint |
| $\lambda_{im\sigma}^0$ | energy shift generated by the gauge dependece (see section.) |
| $h_{im\sigma}$ | Wiess field acting on the slave spins |
| $Z_{m\sigma}$ | quasiparticle weight, $\langle O_{im\sigma}\rangle^2$ |

## Model Hamiltonian

We consider the multi-orbital Hubbard model with density-density interactions,

$$
H = \sum_{ij,m,\sigma} t_{ij}\, d^{\dagger}_{im\sigma} d_{jm\sigma}
- \mu \sum_{i,m,\sigma} n_{im\sigma} + H_{int},
$$

with $n_{im\sigma} = d^{\dagger}_{im\sigma} d_{im\sigma}$ and

$$
H_{int} = U \sum_{i,m} n_{im\uparrow} n_{im\downarrow}
+ U' \sum_{i,\, m \neq m'} n_{im\uparrow} n_{im'\downarrow}
+ (U' - J) \sum_{i,\, m < m',\, \sigma} n_{im\sigma} n_{im'\sigma}.
$$

We use the rotationally invariant relation $U' = U - 2J$ and neglect crystal-field splitting. The spin-flip and pair-hopping terms of the full Kanamori interaction are not included; only terms built from densities appear. This is what allows the interaction to be written entirely in terms of $S^z$ below.

## Slave-spin representation

1. **Hilbert-space enlargement.** the mapping

$$
d_{im\sigma} \rightarrow f_{im\sigma}\, O_{im\sigma}, \qquad
d^{\dagger}_{im\sigma} \rightarrow O^{\dagger}_{im\sigma} f^{\dagger}_{im\sigma}.
$$

2. **Define $O$:** $O_{im\sigma} = S^-_{im\sigma} + c_{im\sigma} S^+_{im\sigma}$ + the expression of the gauge parameter $c_{im\sigma}$ and stress that it is chosen to reproduce the noninteracting limit exactly at the mean-field level.

3. **Define the constraint.** The physical subspace satisfies

$$
f^{\dagger}_{im\sigma} f_{im\sigma} = S^z_{im\sigma} + \tfrac{1}{2}.
$$

Mention that this constraint is enforced on average with Lagrange multipliers $\lambda_{im\sigma}$ by adding

$$
H_{\lambda} = \sum_{i,m,\sigma} \lambda_{im\sigma}
\left( f^{\dagger}_{im\sigma} f_{im\sigma} - S^z_{im\sigma} - \tfrac{1}{2} \right)
$$

to the Hamiltonian.
